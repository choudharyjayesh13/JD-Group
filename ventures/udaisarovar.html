About

Opened in November 2024, The Udaisarovar is JD Group's flagship resort — a lakeside paradise at Lakadwas on the banks of Udai Sagar, twenty minutes from Udaipur city. Four luxury cottages and a spacious family room sit right on the waterline, with an infinity pool, garden lawns, bonfire nights, camping and home-cooked Rajasthani food.

The resort has quickly become one of east Udaipur's favourite venues for weddings, phere ceremonies, Holi parties, DJ nights and corporate offsites, with complete stage, décor and production handled by the group's own events team. Guests praise the peaceful lakeside setting, the still water and the Aravalli backdrop — a slower pace, minutes from the city.
